What the baseline shows
  • 13 issues, 2,681 page views. That is about 206 views per issue.
  • 50 clicks reached RKR pages (cpp.edu/rkr), a pooled click rate of 1.9%.
  • 2 issues had zero RKR clicks. The best issue reached 5.9%.
  • 41% of views came from mobile, so the RKR section has to work on a phone screen.

This is the pre-test baseline. The Fall test moves the “Reserve a Table” button to the upper left and compares new issues against these numbers.
